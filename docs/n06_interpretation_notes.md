## PROB interpretation (pedagogical observation, not statistical)
- Average learner performance for PROB is the lowest (32%), but DBE error
  mentions are among the lowest (13).
- Hypothesis: PROB is under-taught in schools because teachers perceive it
  as easy. Learners get less class time and less practice.
- Consequence for priority engine: PROB still ranks high for intervention,
  but the intervention is "increase exposure" not "clarify misconception".
- This is a teacher-practice hypothesis. It is not measured in DBE data.
  It should be flagged as such and, ideally, validated with teacher
  interviews or a school survey in a later phase.
