## Methodology & Limitations

### Measuring sticks

**Mastery tiers (layered — each tier makes a distinct claim):**

| Tier | Claim | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Attempted only | No claim |
| L1 | Passed once | Weak |
| L2 | Passed original AND unseen variant | Moderate — method proven, not memorization |
| L3 | Passed again 7+ days later | Strong — retention |
| L4-provisional | Two 7+ day passes within 14 days + variant | Provisional mastery — retention pending |
| D | Retention check passed at day 30+ | Durable mastery — claim verified |

**Statistical methods:**
- **Confidence intervals:** Wilson score interval for binomial proportions (more accurate than Wald at small n and extreme rates).
- **Bayesian posterior:** Beta(1,1) prior, updated with each attempt. Reported as 95% credible interval — the *probability range* for the true skill rate.
- **Trajectory:** Ordinary least squares on per-attempt correctness. Slope reported with 95% CI and p-value. Non-significant trends are marked.
- **Cohort comparison:** Chi-square test of homogeneity. Assumes independent observations — a simplifying assumption that may be violated by repeated attempts on the same question.
- **Passing rule (lenient):** An attempt counts as passing if the math grader confirms the answer is correct (error_type ∈ {none, match}). Presentation-only errors are counted as passing but flagged in the tutor voice.

### What this report does NOT claim

- **No attribution of improvement to the tutor.** Without a control group, we cannot say the tutor caused the improvement.
- **Provisional mastery is not durable mastery.** An L4-provisional claim means the learner met the operational threshold under policy v1_2026_10. A durable (D) claim requires an unassisted retention check passed at day 30+. The two are distinguished throughout this report.
- **Missed retention checks are marked missing, not failed.** Follow-up attrition is tracked explicitly because returning learners may differ systematically from those who don't.
- **No prediction of exam outcomes.** Trajectory slopes are estimates, not forecasts.
- **No causal inference from cohort comparison.** Populations differ (voluntary app users vs full national cohort).
- **No significance claims without multiple-comparison caveats.** Running 20 statistical tests, one will be significant by chance.
